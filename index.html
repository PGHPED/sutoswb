01 — Our story

The Company

Founded November 12, 2023.

Sutos Co. started on November 12, 2023 — founded by a 12-year-old who just couldn't stop thinking about tech. No boardroom, no pitch deck. Just a notebook, some free time after school, and one idea: build things instead of only using what everyone else made.

From day one it's been about learning for real — apps that solve small problems, games that mess with new ideas, and AI trained to explore what tech can actually mean for people. Every line of code is a lesson: write it, break it, fix it, repeat.

The philosophy's simple: you learn by building. You don't need to know everything to start, or have years of experience — just enough curiosity to jump in, and the stubbornness to keep going even when you're starting from literally nothing.
